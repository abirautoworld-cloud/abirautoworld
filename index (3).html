Documentation support

Paperwork guidance, explained clearly.

We help customers understand and coordinate the documentation commonly involved in buying, selling and exchanging a pre-owned vehicle.

The exact documents depend on the vehicle, state, RTO and transaction. Official approval, verification and statutory processing are handled by the relevant authorities. Please confirm the specific service and any applicable charges with us before proceeding.

RC / Registration CertificateCheck and coordinate RC-related paperwork and ownership transfer.
Form 29 & Form 30Guidance on the commonly used sale and transfer forms, where applicable.
Insurance documentsCheck policy details and understand transfer or renewal requirements.
PUC certificateCheck the Pollution Under Control certificate status and validity.
Loan / hypothecationGuidance on loan-closure proof and hypothecation removal paperwork, if applicable.
NOC / interstate transferInformation on NOC and related steps when a vehicle moves between states, if required.
Tax & challan checksReminders to verify road-tax status and pending traffic challans.
Buyer / seller KYCChecklist for identity and address proof required for the transaction.
